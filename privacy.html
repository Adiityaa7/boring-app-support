Privacy policy for Boring App, maintained by @Adiityaa7.

Effective November 3, 2026 · Native app version 1.0

Camera and eye tracking

Eye tracking is optional. If you enable it, the app requests camera permission and processes camera frames on your device using Apple’s ARKit or Vision frameworks. It estimates facial landmarks and gaze direction to provide a gentle reminder when you look away from the focus dot.

The app does not record, upload, share, or save camera images, face landmarks, or gaze coordinates. Calibration samples exist only in memory and are discarded when tracking stops. The app does not identify you or create a biometric identity profile.

The camera stops when you switch tracking off, finish a session, leave the Focus screen, or put the app in the background. You may also revoke camera permission in your device’s system settings. Activity timers use no camera. Dot practice uses camera setup for look-away reminders.

Information stored on your device

The app saves your preferences and session records locally. A record contains an identifier, start date, activity, chosen duration, time recorded, completion or early-finish status, pause count, whether tracking was enabled, reminder count, and any optional focus reflection you choose. An active or paused activity timer is saved locally so it can resume after reopening. Optional local notifications use your device’s notification system to announce timer completion. There is no cloud sync or developer-operated account.

You can delete session history in Settings. Removing the app deletes its regular local data according to your operating system’s behavior. Free-access start and last-used dates are stored separately in the device Keychain to manage the 24-hour free period. Keychain records may remain after reinstalling and are not removed by clearing practice history.

Subscriptions

Apple handles subscription purchases, payment details, renewal, and refunds. The app uses StoreKit to verify purchase status and determine access. We do not receive your payment card details. Subscription management is available in your Apple account settings.

No advertising or analytics

The app includes no third-party advertising or analytics SDKs and does not send session history, camera data, or gaze data to the developer. Apple may collect App Store or operating-system diagnostics according to your Apple settings and Apple’s privacy policy.

This website and support

These public pages are hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub processes website requests under its own privacy statement. This site adds no analytics, advertising, or tracking scripts.

If you open a support issue on GitHub, the information you submit is public and is processed by GitHub. Share only what is necessary to describe the problem. Do not post camera images, payment details, health information, or other sensitive data.

Changes and contact

We will update this page if the app’s data practices change and revise the effective date. For privacy questions, contact the maintainer through the Boring App support repository. If your question requires private communication, request a private contact channel without including sensitive information in the public issue.
