A GOOD KIND OF NOTHING.

A little less noise.
A moment for you.

Boring App is a native app for iPhone, iPad, and Mac. Follow one large, slowly moving dot for 30 seconds, 1, 2, or 5 minutes. Optional on-device eye tracking offers a gentle reminder when you look away.

No feed. No scores. No streak to protect. Just a small practice in staying with one thing.

In development

The app is not yet available on the App Store. Planned pricing is 24 hours of free app access followed by an optional $4.99 USD annual subscription. Starting free access does not authorize a charge. Final prices are shown in the App Store purchase sheet.

Private by nature

Camera images and gaze estimates stay on your device. Camera use is optional. On devices without usable haptics, sound and a visual cue provide a gentle reminder.

This is a focus exercise, not a medical treatment or a validated measure of attention. Tracking is an estimate and can be inaccurate.
