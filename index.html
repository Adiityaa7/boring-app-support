Boring App is a native app for iPhone, iPad, and Mac. Follow one large, slowly moving dot with short presets for settling before maths, memory work or deep focus. On-device eye-direction estimates offer a prominent reminder when you look away during dot practice.

No feed or streak to protect. A session recap shows time, reminders and an estimated on-screen score when tracking coverage is sufficient.

In development

The app is not yet available on the App Store. The app is being prepared for Apple review. Membership is $9.99 USD per year, with a 3-day free introductory trial for eligible new subscribers. The trial begins after confirmation with Apple and renews automatically unless canceled at least 24 hours before it ends. Local prices and eligibility are shown before purchase.

Private by nature

Camera images and gaze estimates stay on your device. Camera use is optional. On devices without usable haptics, sound and a visual cue provide a prominent reminder.

This is a focus exercise, not a medical treatment or a validated measure of attention. Tracking is an estimate and can be inaccurate.
