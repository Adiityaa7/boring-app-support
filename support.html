A little help.

Boring App is currently in development for iPhone, iPad, and Mac. App Store release has not yet occurred.

Routines and progress — local build 9

Routines connect a short dot warm-up, a work timer and a break. Choose work, maths, revision, chess or an ADHD-friendly small start. Each stage waits for you to continue. “Start with work” explicitly skips the camera stage. Save favorite routines, add an optional intention, and save a private note in the recap. Custom timers allow 1–10 minutes of dot practice or 1–90 minutes of work.

Progress shows your last seven days, a personal daily goal and an estimated on-screen percentage from qualifying dot sessions. Breaks do not inflate practice totals. You can filter session history and export it as CSV. These are records of time and camera estimates, not proof of improved attention or task performance.

Activity timers and recaps

Choose Maths, Office work, Chess, Reading or Study. Office work defaults to 20 minutes. These timers are for work in another app or away from the screen, so the camera stays off. Timers continue when switching apps and restore after reopening. Allow notifications for completion alerts while away; notification delivery also follows system Focus and sound settings. The recap records time and pauses, plus an optional focus reflection you choose. It does not measure work quality or attention.

Dot presets and scores

The development build offers Quick reset (1 min), Before maths (3 min), Memory work (4 min), Deep focus (5 min) and an ADHD-friendly Small start (2 min). These are sessions following the same slowly moving dot before another activity, not maths questions, memory tests or a treatment. Durations are adjustable. Work timers remain available separately.

A dot recap shows time completed, reminder count and an estimated on-screen score: the percentage of measured time classified as eyes on screen. It needs at least 10 seconds of session data and 80% tracking coverage. Unclear readings are excluded and reduce coverage; insufficient coverage withholds the score. It does not measure task performance, memory ability, clinical attention or exact fixation on the dot.

Eye setup isn’t finishing

Development builds 8 and 9 replace the multi-position calibration with a 10-second eye check and a visible countdown. Choose Follow the dot, tap Begin and allow camera access. Rest your eyes on the dot for two seconds, then glance off-screen with your head facing forward when prompted. A detectable eyes-only glance must trigger the normal reminder; look back before the countdown ends. A successful eye check and working audio playback start the moving-dot timer automatically at 10 seconds, with no extra confirmation button. A failed check stops at 10 seconds with a retry message. If the camera cannot distinguish the glance, the session stays stopped and offers another attempt. Even light, a comfortable distance and visible eyes help. This build remains under validation and has not been released. Mac and physical iPhone tracking must pass before release.

I don’t feel a vibration

First tap “Test reminder” to check the output separately from tracking. Keep media volume audible. After a 1.2-second grace period, alerts repeat every 2 seconds while you look away, stop when you return, and stop when the timer ends or pauses. Haptics depend on device hardware. Supported iPhones can vibrate. Mac and iPad use a prominent paired-tone sound and an on-screen cue. In Settings, enable sound and haptics as desired. The Mac itself does not vibrate. Build 9 pauses dot practice if reminder audio fails instead of continuing silently.

Free access and membership

Local Debug previews unlock sessions for testing and disable purchases. Release builds retain the membership described below. No price change or public release has been made.

Your free day begins with your first session and lasts 24 hours on that device. It does not automatically charge you. After it ends, an annual subscription is required to begin more sessions. The planned US price is $4.99 per year; the purchase sheet displays the actual local price. A purchased subscription renews until canceled.

Use “Restore purchases” under Membership if you already subscribed with the same Apple account. To cancel or manage a subscription, open Apple subscriptions. For refunds, use Apple’s purchase support.

Report a problem

Open a support issue with your device model, operating-system version, app version, and a short description of what happened.

GitHub issues are public. Please do not include camera photos, purchase receipts, passwords, or other sensitive information.

About this practice

The app offers a simple focus exercise. It does not diagnose or treat a condition, guarantee improved attention, or produce a validated attention score.
