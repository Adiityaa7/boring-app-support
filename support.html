A little help.

Boring App is currently in development for iPhone, iPad, and Mac. App Store release has not yet occurred.

Activity timers and recaps

Choose Maths, Office work, Chess, Reading or Study. Office work defaults to 20 minutes. These timers are for work in another app or away from the screen, so the camera stays off. Timers continue when switching apps and restore after reopening. Allow notifications for completion alerts while away; notification delivery also follows system Focus and sound settings. The recap records time and pauses, plus an optional focus reflection you choose. It does not measure work quality or attention.

Eye tracking isn’t working

In the current development build (version 1.0, build 5), choose Dot practice, tap Begin, and allow camera access. The timer starts after setup. Check for “Camera on · eyes detected”. If permission was denied, use Camera settings and retry. Sit in even light, keep your head fairly still, and follow each calibration dot; setup advances automatically without tapping. Glasses, camera quality, distance, and lighting can affect results. Tracking is an estimate. Activity timers work with the camera off. If eye calibration is unreliable, “Use face-direction reminders” detects turning away or leaving the camera. It cannot detect eyes-only glances. Camera and vibration testing requires a physical device, not the iOS simulator.

I don’t feel a vibration

First tap “Test reminder” to check the output separately from tracking. Keep media volume audible. After a 1.2-second grace period, alerts repeat every 2 seconds while you look away, stop when you return, and stop when the timer ends or pauses. Haptics depend on device hardware. Supported iPhones can vibrate. Mac and iPad use a prominent paired-tone sound and an on-screen cue. In Settings, enable sound and haptics as desired. The Mac itself does not vibrate.

Free access and membership

Your free day begins with your first session and lasts 24 hours on that device. It does not automatically charge you. After it ends, an annual subscription is required to begin more sessions. The planned US price is $4.99 per year; the purchase sheet displays the actual local price. A purchased subscription renews until canceled.

Use “Restore purchases” under Membership if you already subscribed with the same Apple account. To cancel or manage a subscription, open Apple subscriptions. For refunds, use Apple’s purchase support.

Report a problem

Open a support issue with your device model, operating-system version, app version, and a short description of what happened.

GitHub issues are public. Please do not include camera photos, purchase receipts, passwords, or other sensitive information.

About this practice

The app offers a simple focus exercise. It does not diagnose or treat a condition, guarantee improved attention, or produce a validated attention score.
